FOR THE KWASU COMPUTING COMMUNITY

A Structured Way to Be Heard

The Student Complaint Portal provides a central platform where students can communicate concerns relating to academic support, welfare, student representation and other chapter matters. The goal is to make communication clearer, organized and easier to follow.

AREAS OF CONCERN

What Can You Report?

The portal can be used for matters affecting students and the computing community.

O

Other Chapter Matters

Submit other relevant concerns requiring attention from representatives.

IMPORTANT

Built for Students, Built for Accountability.

This portal is intended to provide a clear channel for students to communicate with their representatives. Students should provide accurate information when submitting a complaint.

Before submitting a complaint

Make sure the information you provide is accurate and relevant to the issue being reported. Avoid submitting duplicate reports for the same concern.
